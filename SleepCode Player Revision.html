SleepCode Player Revision

SleepCode player revision

Visual reference for Cursor. Uses the live site's tokens (--bg #10131a, --accent #adc4e8, Instrument Sans, IBM Plex Mono). Frames are 390 × 844 (iPhone). Numbered pins match the notes under each frame. The written spec is in sleepcode-player-revision-spec.md.

Mix stays on the playerOne fader, Voice ↔ current background. The right-hand label is whatever is selected: Pulse, a nature sound, or a music title.
Level is removedVolume is the phone's own control. Keep a fixed internal gain so existing listeners don't get louder.
Two icon CTAs replace three tilesSettings and Length, line icons with no boxes. The set value shows under the icon in the accent color.
Settings sheetNarrator, Background (opens a leaf page), Repeat, Spacing.
Background leaf pageTabs Pulse · Nature · Music. Pulse = 4 stereo widths under the subhead "Bilateral audio stimulation". Nature (6) and Music (12) have a preview button per row.
Removed from the playerLength / Sound / Repeat tiles, the "Owen · Change" link, the Width control as a separate setting.
